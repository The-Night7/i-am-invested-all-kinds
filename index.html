The Girls

Rumi — the one who was already there. Spent a whole morning researching snacks for the car. Cannot eat any of them. Waited outside anyway.

Celine — the one holding it all together. A Sunlight Sister. Told Rumi to wait inside three times. Rumi was outside anyway.

The Headcanon (by polytrixaf)

"zoey has never had an easy time getting along with people her age — she knows she's a lot; she gets loud when she's excited, she has fixations no one else really cares about, she touches for too long."

"mira could get along with anyone, if she had to, but lately she doesn't want to. she's spent her whole life working to win people over... now she's determined to be her own person — except she isn't sure she knows who that is."

And then they meet Rumi. Who is immediately, blatantly, harmlessly weird — and who makes it very clear by the end of the first day that she does not know how to be a normal teenager, desperately wants to be liked, and has very strict personal boundaries.

It's going to be just fine.
